# Pulse project delivery

Pulse connects team delivery work with customer projects and recorded effort.

## Language

**Project**: A coordinated body of work with members, dates and an optional customer or sales order.

**Task**: An accepted unit of project work, which may have subtasks, dependencies, estimates and recorded time.
_Avoid_: Request, ticket (when referring to unaccepted intake)

**Request**: Incoming work awaiting acceptance, rejection, deferral or a duplicate decision. Acceptance creates a task while retaining the request history.

**Board state**: A project-configured stage of work, such as review. A review state does not by itself mean the task is blocked.

**Blocked task**: Open work held up by an unresolved dependency or an explicit blocked state.

**Time entry**: A dated interval of actual work attributable to a person and task, marked billable or nonbillable.

**Sprint**: A dated planning period with a goal, committed scope and recorded changes to that scope.
